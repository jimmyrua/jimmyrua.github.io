THE PRODUCT CHALLENGE

The answer is somewhere.
Finding it is the work.

Employees need clear answers from scattered policies. Knowledge teams need control over what is published and what happens when an answer falls short.

The tool connects both sides: reviewed knowledge, answers with sources, and a feedback loop that turns recurring questions into maintained knowledge.

THREE CONNECTED JOURNEYS

Manage → Answer → Improve
01

MANAGE TRUSTED KNOWLEDGE

A document becomes
a reliable source.

Import PDF, Word or Markdown, confirm extracted metadata, then review and publish. Revisions keep knowledge current; archived or expired policies leave the answer flow.

DESIGN DECISION

Keep the current version available while its replacement is reviewed.

02

ANSWER WITH SOURCES

An answer you can
check for yourself.

Employees ask in natural language. Retrieval checks publication status, validity and role access before the response cites a policy version and passage.

DESIGN DECISION

Keep source evidence visible; exclude expired or unauthorized content.

03

IMPROVE THE KNOWLEDGE BASE

Today’s open question.
Tomorrow’s shared answer.

Unanswered questions reach human support. Similar unresolved questions are grouped for review, then developed into FAQs with policy evidence.

DESIGN DECISION

Suggestions become reusable knowledge only after evidence binding and human approval.
