    METHOD & SOURCES

    Make the score explainable.

    Each component is expressed on a 0–1 percentile scale across all 158 neighbourhoods. The displayed total is scaled to 0–100.

    Health need
    Percentile rank of the project’s age-standardized mental health and addiction (MHA) visit rate.
    Social disconnection
    Mean percentile rank of people living alone and the low-income measure.
    Access gap
    Mean of nearest-facility distance percentile and the inverse facility-count percentile. Distance starts at a representative point inside each neighbourhood, using the original R project’s EPSG:3978 projection.

    Default score: 45% need + 35% disconnection + 20% access gap. A higher score signals greater relative priority under those weights, not a medical risk diagnosis. Priority colour bands: <25, 25–<50, 50–<75, 75–100. Ties use the minimum percentile rank; ranking ties use the higher visit rate.

    Limits: Descriptive, cross-sectional project data. Reference dates for health and facility records were not supplied. Inventory counts do not measure capacity, appointment availability or waiting times. Straight-line distance is not transit access. The map uses simplified 2022 neighbourhood boundaries; tilt and lift are presentation effects. Use Flat map for geographic comparison.

    Provenance: The supplied STA313 files integrate OCHPP health profiles, City of Toronto boundaries, and Statistics Canada’s Open Database of Healthcare Facilities. Facility classifications and aggregated counts are retained from that project. No live data refresh is claimed.
