BEHIND THE PROJECT

Useful knowledge
needs structure.

Research often ends up scattered across documents, notes and conversations. JimmyAI brings structure to that work: retain the source, organize what matters, and make the next action explicit.

01 / TRACEABILITY

A conclusion needs a source.

Keep findings connected to the material behind them, with limitations visible.

02 / REUSABILITY

A document can become a tool.

Organize research into cards and tags that support later retrieval and comparison.

03 / REVIEW

Progress stays inspectable.

Separate the proposed work, the supporting evidence and the decision to proceed.
