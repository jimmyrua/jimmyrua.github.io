ABOUT THE PROJECT

A CRM analytics project connecting customer ratings, financial signals and risk events to explain account changes and guide follow-up. The analysis moves from rating audits to exploratory risk prioritization and customer value segmentation, using Python and structured business rules.

THE BUSINESS QUESTION

Does every downgrade
tell the right story?

Manual rating adjustments influence customer prioritization and resource allocation. This analysis connects each change to financial signals and risk events, highlighting decisions that deserve a closer look.

03 / EXPLORATORY RISK PRIORITIZATION

Test the rules before trusting the queue.

Reported analysis

Nine business rules combine financial, inventory, event, visit and remediation signals into a weighted score out of 13. Thresholds of 8+ and 6–7 define higher and middle trigger bands; these are proposed business rules, not calibrated risk probabilities.

COVERAGE70.0–100%

Availability differs by rule.

Visit data covers 70.0% of accounts; inventory covers 85.9%. Missing signals must remain visible rather than imply low risk.

CO-TRIGGER CHECK77.7%

Some signals may count twice.

Operational and inventory flags co-trigger for 3,332 accounts. Overlap calls for checking whether rules represent distinct evidence.

SENSITIVITY TEST44%

The top 100 changes sharply.

Only 44 accounts remain in both top-100 lists when remediation-pool membership is removed. At fixed thresholds, 613 accounts change bands.

The remediation pool may already reflect existing risk events. Confirm how membership is assigned before treating it as independent evidence. Weights and thresholds still need business validation.

04 / EXPLORATORY CUSTOMER VALUE

Reported analysis

Recommended actions, not measured business outcomes.
