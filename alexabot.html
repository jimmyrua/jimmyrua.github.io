THE PRODUCT CHALLENGE

The answer is somewhere.
Finding it is the work.

Employees need clear answers from scattered policies. Knowledge teams need control over what is published and what happens when an answer falls short.

AlexaBot connects both sides: reviewed knowledge, answers with sources, and a path to human support.

THREE CONNECTED JOURNEYS

Publish → Answer → Resolve
01

PUBLISH TRUSTED KNOWLEDGE

A document becomes
a reliable source.

Materials move through parsing, review and publication before they become answerable knowledge.

DESIGN DECISION

Keep the draft separate from the version employees can use.

02

ANSWER WITH SOURCES

An answer you can
check for yourself.

Employees ask in natural language. Relevant policy evidence supports the response, with a reference back to the source.

DESIGN DECISION

Put the supporting passage within reach of the answer.

03

RESOLVE UNANSWERED QUESTIONS

Keep the conversation
moving forward.

When the available knowledge is insufficient, clarification or human follow-up provides a path forward.

DESIGN DECISION

Carry the question and conversation context into the handoff.
